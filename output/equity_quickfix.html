Quickfix strategy · daily · $100k starting capital

Quickfix equity curve optimized

Takes profit at a fixed ceiling: a trade is closed at 1.9R, or earlier at the first opposite reversal that sits between entry and 1.9R. The 1.9R ceiling caps every winner. All 41 markets in the archive were tested, Dec 2025 – Jul 2026; the rules fired on 28 of them, and the rest are listed with zero trades under By market. Capital moves only when a trade closes; each new trade risks 1.39% of liquid capital, the risk that holds this strategy to a 6% maximum drawdown. Figures are net of realistic slippage.

How a trade is found
Described for a short; the long side is the exact mirror, bearish levels for the entry, bullish for the target.
1
Rule 1 · Signal
At least 3 bullish reversal levels lie between the previous day's close and today's high, price rose into them and tested them. The lowest of those is the first reversal, the next one up the second.
2
Rule 2 · Clean setup
Refuse the trade if the bar opened at or above the second reversal. Opening below the first, or between the first and the second, is fine; opening above the second means price was already inside the cluster when the day began.
3
Rule 3 · Room to move
The nearest bearish reversal below the entry must be at least 3.5R below it. Any closer and there is not enough room to pay for the risk being taken.
4
Rule 4 · Target
Take profit at 1.9R. If a bearish reversal sits closer than 1.9R below the entry, that level becomes the target instead, price is likely to turn there rather than run through it. 1.9R is a hard ceiling on every winner.
Entry and stop. The bar must close below the first reversal, the proof that price probed the cluster and snapped back. The trade fills at that reversal price, and the stop sits one tick above that bar's high. Position size makes that distance exactly 1.39% of capital, so 1R = 1.39%. One position per market at a time; management starts the day after entry.
Entries come from the reversal levels only. No timing, cycle or aggregate turning-point signal is involved, this is the price half of the Socrates method on its own.
