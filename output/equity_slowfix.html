Same entries as quickfix, held longer: there is no 5R ceiling, so a trade runs to the first opposite reversal beyond entry however far away that is. Rule 3 keeps that level at least 3.5R away at entry. One account trading the Socrates time-and-price reversal signals across 28 markets, Dec 2025 – Jul 2026. Capital moves only when a trade closes; each new trade risks 1% of liquid capital. Figures are net of realistic slippage.

Backtest, net of slippage. Costs are charged as tick slippage — 1 tick on entry, 1 on a limit take-profit, 3 on a stop — converted to R through each trade's own risk distance (about $5.2k of drag here, gross was +215.1%). Still optimistic on the rest: the entry-day intraday path is assumed favorable, a reversal target counts as hit whenever the day's range touches it, and there is no commission or funding. Read it as evidence of an edge, not a return forecast.
